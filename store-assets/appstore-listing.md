# App Store Listing — The Memory Palace

## English (U.S.)

**App Name:** The Memory Palace
**Subtitle:** Your 3D Memory Palace
**Keywords:** memory palace,family tree,memories,3d,loci method,genealogy,legacy,memory preservation,family legacy

**Description:**
Your family's stories are worth more than a photo album. Build the place where they truly come alive.

The Memory Palace transforms your most precious memories into an immersive 3D experience. Walk through beautifully designed rooms filled with your family's photos, voice recordings, and stories — in a virtual palace that feels like home.

FEATURES:
- Walk through a stunning 3D Tuscan villa filled with your memories
- Organize photos, videos, and voice recordings into themed rooms
- Build your family tree and connect generations
- AI-powered interviews that help capture family stories
- Share rooms and memories with family members
- Legacy mode to preserve stories for future generations
- Available in English, Dutch, German, Spanish, and French

HOW IT WORKS:
1. Create your free palace
2. Upload photos, videos, and voice recordings
3. Walk through your rooms in immersive 3D
4. Invite family members to contribute and explore together

Your memories deserve a palace, not a folder.

**What's New (v1.2.0):**

- Kep: capture memories via WhatsApp — forward photos and stories directly to your palace
- Cinematic flyover when you first enter your palace
- Improved 3D performance on mobile devices
- Family tree visual improvements
- Bug fixes and stability improvements

**Promotional Text:** Step inside your family's story. Build a 3D palace of memories, record voices, grow your tree. Start preserving today.
