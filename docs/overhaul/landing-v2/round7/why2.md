# "Why a palace?" — FINAL block (round 7)

## Decision

**One pinned `band-together.jpg` hero. The image never changes; only the argument deepens beneath it, across three short scroll beats: LOCI, then DIGNITY (everyone already has a palace), then ENRICH.**

All three judging panels converge on the single-asset, three-beat pinned sequence (Idea 2) as the winner, and every one of them independently killed the multi-image concepts (Ideas 1 and 3) on VISUAL grounds. I verified this on the real pixels:

- `band-together.jpg` — deep warm amber golden-hour, heavy umber shadow, the reaching hand pressed to a framed photo, an archway of light, dust motes. Premium, and it carries the whole argument on its own. This is the round-6 winner and the only color-consistent, premium asset on disk.
- `band-entrance.jpg` — COOL, desaturated gray-cream rotunda in bright neutral light, with a baked-in "Skip intro" UI button top-right and "ROOTS" / "SIONS" placard text. It is a raw game screenshot, not a clean render.
- `band-corridor.jpg` — COOL neutral gray/beige, bright, with a baked-in "ROOTS" placard. Also clearly a screenshot (added Jul 17).

A cross-fade from cool, bright, text-stamped screenshots into a deep-amber hero would read as a mismatched slideshow and look cheap, which is exactly the constructed, diagram-y failure the last two rounds fought off. There is no fix short of re-grading and de-lettering two 3D screenshots, which is out of scope for a CSS block. So we reuse ONLY `band-together.jpg`. It cannot suffer a color mismatch, because there is nothing to mismatch.

Why the three-beat sequence beats the round-6 single static line: it weaves in the three ideas the brief demands (method of loci, everyone-has-a-palace, enrich) without adding a second pixel or asking a 68-year-old to decode a busy composite. One trusted picture holds still; the reader receives one clear thought at a time, the way you would tell a story across a dinner table. The beloved round-6 payoff ("You walk up and touch it") is kept intact as the final beat, so nothing already loved is lost.

Guardrails carried from the panels:
- Three beats is the ceiling. A fourth beat, or an eyebrow on every beat, tips dignified into busy. Do not add one.
- The block is fully legible at rest. Beat 1 shows with zero motion; scrolling is a gift, never a hurdle.
- "Peasant or president" is never run bare. It is always anchored by the load-bearing dignity line ("Your life is worth keeping"), the emotional core of the whole set, per all three panels. Where the phrase risks reading flip for a dignified reader, the softer "Rich or humble, near or far" is the sanctioned fallback.
- Never hijack the wheel. Scroll progress only maps to opacity. Every pause point is fully readable, never a half-empty block.

## VISUAL — implementable spec

React + CSS/SVG only. No three.js. All sizing in `rem`; positional overlays in `%`. One image on disk, reused.

### Asset
- **Single hero, reused three times over:** `/landing/band-together.jpg` (verified present, premium deep-amber golden-hour, reaching hand + frames intact). This is the ONLY image in the block. No `band-corridor.jpg`, no `band-entrance.jpg`, no `hero-poster.jpg`, no demo thumbnails, no composite, no SVG line-art. The image never swaps; only the copy beats cross-fade.

### Section structure (the pin)
- Outer `<section>` is the scroll track. **Desktop:** `min-height: 250vh` (about 2 to 2.5 viewports, deliberately short so it never feels stalled). **Mobile (≤48rem) and under `prefers-reduced-motion`:** the section collapses to one normal-height figure (`min-height: auto`), no pin, all three sub-lines rendered stacked under the constant H2.
- Inside, the `<figure role="img" aria-label="…">` is `position: sticky; top: 50%; transform: translateY(-50%);` (vertically centered pin) on desktop; `position: static` on mobile / reduced-motion.
- Figure frame (identical recipe to round 6, reads as a hung picture):
  - `position: relative; overflow: hidden;`
  - `max-width: 64rem; margin-inline: auto;`
  - `border-radius: 1.25rem;`
  - `border: 1px solid #E3D6BC;` (hairline)
  - `box-shadow: 0 1.5rem 3rem rgba(36, 28, 21, 0.18);` (soft umber lift off `#FCFAF5`)
  - Section canvas `#FCFAF5`, `padding-block: clamp(3rem, 6vw, 5rem)`, `padding-inline: clamp(1rem, 4vw, 2rem)`.
  - Aspect: desktop `aspect-ratio: 16 / 9`; mobile (≤48rem) `aspect-ratio: 4 / 5`.

### Image element
- `next/image` `fill` (or `<img>` with `object-fit: cover`).
- `object-position: 68% 42%;` desktop (**load-bearing** — pins the reaching hand + frames). Mobile (≤48rem) `object-position: 72% 40%;`. Verify the hand + at least one frame survive at 375px or the "touch" metaphor collapses.
- `sizes="(max-width: 48rem) 100vw, 64rem"`, `priority={false}`, lazy.

### Scrim (MANDATORY — not decorative)
Two stacked gradients as a `::after` overlay, confined to the naturally-dark left + lower-left where the text sits, so the sunlit right (hand + frames + arch) stays untouched. Exact recipe carried from the shipped build:

```css
figure::after {
  content: "";
  position: absolute; inset: 0;
  background:
    linear-gradient(105deg,
      rgba(36, 28, 21, 0.74) 0%,
      rgba(36, 28, 21, 0.34) 40%,
      transparent 66%),
    linear-gradient(to top,
      rgba(36, 28, 21, 0.55) 0%,
      transparent 42%);
  pointer-events: none;
}
```
- Base alpha ≥0.72 at the lower-left origin is non-negotiable. The amber wall behind the text well is the brightest region, so if the cream H2 washes out, DEEPEN the scrim; never shrink the type. Verify **AA (≥4.5:1)** on the real `band-together` pixels at 375 / 768 / 1280px.

### Text well (bottom-left, constant frame + swapping beat)
- `position: absolute; left: 0; bottom: 0;` padding desktop `2.5rem`, mobile `1.5rem`. `max-width: 30rem` desktop / `22rem` mobile.
- Stacking order top to bottom, with the top three CONSTANT across all beats so the reader is never re-anchored:
  1. **Eyebrow** — Caveat (`--font-note`), gold `#D4AF37`, `1.15rem`, letter-spacing `0.02em`. Constant.
  2. **Gold progress hairline** — the ONLY ornament, and it doubles as the 3-beat progress cue. `height: 1px; background: #D4AF37; opacity: 0.4; margin-block: 0.75rem;` Its `width` animates from `2.5rem` at progress 0 to the full text-well width at progress 1 (`width: calc(2.5rem + (100% - 2.5rem) * var(--progress))`), so the reader feels they are walking somewhere. Under reduced motion it renders at a fixed `2.5rem`.
  3. **H2** — Cormorant italic (`--font-display`), cream `#FCFAF5`, `clamp(1.75rem, 4.5vw, 3rem)`, `line-height: 1.05`, `text-shadow: 0 1px 12px rgba(36, 28, 21, 0.45)`. Constant.
  4. **Beat lead** — Cormorant italic (`--font-display`), cream `#FCFAF5`, `clamp(1.15rem, 2vw, 1.5rem)`, `margin-top: 1rem`. Swaps per beat.
  5. **Beat sub** — Manrope (`--font-body`), `#EFE6D4` at `opacity: 0.92`, `clamp(1rem, 1.4vw, 1.0625rem)`, `line-height: 1.5`, `margin-top: 0.5rem`. Swaps per beat.

### Scroll-to-beat mechanic (no scroll-jacking)
- One `IntersectionObserver` on the section plus a passive `scroll` listener that maps the section's scroll progress to a normalized `0..1` value, held in React state, and to a `--progress` CSS custom property for the hairline. Pure state + CSS `opacity` / `translateY`. No library, never `preventDefault`, never lock or slow the wheel.
- Beat index = `Math.min(2, Math.floor(progress * 3))`. Only the **lead + sub** swap: outgoing beat `opacity 1 → 0`, incoming `opacity 0 → 1` + `translateY(0.4rem → 0)` over `0.4s ease`. At any pause point exactly one beat is fully rendered; there is never a half-empty block.
- Near-frame Caveat label and hand-caption cross-fade in sync with their beat (Beat 1 label, Beat 2 hand-caption), desktop only.

### Motion (single restrained Ken Burns, on the final beat only)
- During Beat 3 (progress ≥ 0.66), the image does the existing slow **push-in** `scale(1) → scale(1.05)` over the remaining scroll, `transform-origin: 70% 45%` (drifts toward the reaching hand) as the ENRICH line resolves onto the payoff. Beats 1 and 2 hold `scale(1)`.
- **`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` end state:** no pin, no cross-fade, no push-in. The figure renders once at `scale(1)`; the three beat leads + subs render as a static stacked list beneath the constant H2; the Body summary is shown. Fully legible in under 5s with zero motion.

### Mobile (≤48rem)
- No pin, no scroll-jack. The figure renders once (4/5 crop, `object-position: 72% 40%`). Below it, the constant eyebrow + H2, then the three beat leads + subs stacked (or the single Body summary), `max-width: 22rem`. Near-frame label and hand-caption `display: none`.
