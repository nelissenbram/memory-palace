# Final 12 Feature Names — The Palace Household

One coherent system (judges' consensus winner): **The Household** — every feature is a
named member of the palace staff or a room/ritual of the house, in uniform `The ___`
grammar (the Kep pattern: characterful noun + plain descriptor). Built on
Latin/Romance-rooted, translation-safe words. NL = je-form, DE = Sie-form.

## Rationale (why this set won)

- **One system, one grammar.** All 12 keep `The ___`, so the sticky rail reads as a
  single staffed Tuscan house — not a feature list. All three judges named grammatical
  consistency the deciding axis and killed every verb/gerund system (Gather In, Seal &
  Keep, Told Aloud, Handed To You) for breaking the noun-character model and translating
  unevenly.
- **Translation-proof spine.** Deliberately built on cognate/Romance-rooted words that
  survive NL/DE/ES/FR: Steward, Palace, Atlas, Chronicle, Vault, Workshop, Keys.
- **Best-of-breed grafts (all three judges' consensus picks):**
  - `The Steward` — consensus best for AI sort/filing (warm, dignified; Rentmeester/
    Verwalter) over blunt "Sorter" or clinical "Genealogist".
  - `The Chronicle` — for the life-story timeline; more distinctive than "Life Story",
    warmer than "Memoir", cognate everywhere.
  - `The Atlas` — for the map; one concrete cognate noun, more branded than "Memory Map".
  - `The Vault` — for the time capsule; concrete and dignified, beats "Seal"/"Time Capsule".
  - `The Keys` — for legacy/hand-on; tangible and emotional, beats abstract "Bequest"/"Heirs".
  - `The Doorstep` — for import/arrival; warm and picturable, beats "Bring In".
  - `The Post` — for received/shared items; universally known, cleaner than "Mailroom".
  - `The Guest Book` — for visitor sharing; everyone has signed one (Judge 3's clarity pick).
- **One deliberate literal.** `The Family Tree` stays literal — the one feature where
  instant recognition and findability outrank distinctiveness (all judges flagged this;
  "Roots"/"Lineage"/"Bloodline" are prettier but slower, and "Bloodline" reads grim).
- **Brand integrity.** `The Palace` is kept as the core home feature (it IS the product
  name); "Villa"/"House" were rejected as they fragment the brand.
