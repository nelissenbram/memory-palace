### Post 2 — di/wo: de Apple-saga-thread (9 tweets)
1. Apple rejected my app 8 times before letting it in. A thread about the most expensive masterclass in reading comprehension I ever took: 🧵
2. The app: a 3D memory palace for family photos. Harmless, right? Apple's review team disagreed. Eight times. Over five months.
3. Rejection 1–2: IAP errors + OAuth opening in the wrong browser. Fair. Fixed in a week. "This will be quick," I told my wife. Narrator: it was not quick.
4. Rejection 3: iPad layout. The app worked fine on iPad — except in the exact simulator config the reviewer used. Three days to reproduce, one line to fix.
5. Rejection 4 (3.1.1, anti-steering): my pricing page *mentioned* you could subscribe on the web. You may not tell iOS users the web exists. I now understand why every app's settings page feels lobotomized.
6. Rejection 5: made iOS completely free to sidestep 3.1.1. Rejected anyway — because *production hadn't finished deploying* when the reviewer tested. Rejected for a race condition with my own CI.
7. Rejection 6: "app not responsive." Root cause: a WebKit first-responder race after Apple's OWN Sign-in-with-Apple flow returned. Buttons dead until you rotated the phone. Found it with a proof video, frame by frame, at 2am.
8. Lessons: (a) deploy prod BEFORE you submit, (b) the reviewer is always right even when they're wrong, (c) never ship the word "free" near the word "create", (d) solo devs don't have an appeals department — ship boring, argue never.
9. v1.3.0 approved 2026-07-16. I may have cried a little. The palace: https://www.thememorypalace.ai/go/x — ask me anything about App Review, I have scars for every guideline number.
